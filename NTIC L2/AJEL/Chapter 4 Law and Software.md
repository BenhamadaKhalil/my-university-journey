# **Chapter 4: Law and Software**

## **1. Concept of “Software”:**
- **Software** is defined as:
  - A **set of programs, procedures**, and **rules**, and possibly **documentation**, related to a set of **data processing**.
  - The term "**program**" refers to a **set of instructions** designed to perform one or more tasks, solve a problem, or manipulate data.
  - The **program** is the **expression** of an algorithm in a given language for a specific machine.

### **Steps to Develop Software:**
1. **Idea**.
2. **Specifications and overall analysis**.
3. **Detailed analysis** (e.g., using a modeling language like **CORBA** or **UML**).
4. **Source code**.
5. **Executable code**.
6. **Documentation**.

- Software is an **implementation of an idea**, with steps 3, 4, and 5 being the key components.

## **2. Copyright and Software:**
- **Software protection** applies to the **program** itself and any related elements that make it work.
- **User manuals** and **documentation** are not part of the software protection regime; they are subject to the **general protection of literary works**.
- **The law allows reproduction** of software under specific conditions:
  - A **single copy** or adaptation may be made by the lawful owner for **private use**.
  - If the software is lost or damaged, a **replacement copy** can be made for **archival purposes**.

### **Article 52 of the Ordinance n° 03-05 on Copyright and Related Rights**:
- This article permits the **reproduction** or **adaptation** of a **computer program** by the lawful owner of a copy, without permission from the author, under the following conditions:
  1. **For the use of the computer program** for the purpose for which it was acquired and in accordance with the conditions at the time of acquisition.
  2. **For archival purposes**, to replace a legitimately held copy if it is lost, destroyed, or rendered unusable.

- **If the possession of the software becomes unlawful**, the reproduction or adaptation must be destroyed.

- **Salaried Authors**: In the case of salaried authors, the property rights of the software belong to the **employer**, especially when it is created during the employment period or under the employer’s instructions.

## **3. Patent & Software:**
- **Software protection** has been introduced under **copyright law**, but software is generally **excluded from patentability**.
- **Order No. 03-07 of 19 July 2003** explicitly **excludes computer programs from patentability** as inventions under Algerian law.
  
### **When Can Software Be Patented?**
- In principle, **software is patentable** if it is not only the source code, but also includes **technical aspects** and **functionalities** that fulfill the criteria of:
  - **Industrial application**.
  - **Novelty**.
  - **Inventive level**.
  - **Originality**.

## **4. The Rights of the User:**
- Users are granted specific rights by law to use the software, as long as it does not harm the normal exploitation of the software or infringe the legitimate interests of the author.
- **Rights include**:
  - The user can correct **errors (bugs)** in the software unless this right is **contractually reserved** to the author.
  - The user has the right to make a **backup copy** of the software for safekeeping.
  - The user has the right to **study how the software works** to determine its underlying **ideas** and **principles**.
  - The user may **decompile** the program for **interoperability purposes**, provided that the necessary data is not already available in the software documentation or publications, and it is not used to create software that is **substantially similar**.

### **Conditions on Software Distribution:**
1. **Software must not be distributed without a license**.
2. Software components should not be used without knowing their **origin** and **license**.
3. **Licenses** give rights and impose obligations that must be respected.

## **5. Conclusion:**
- The **legal aspects of software** primarily focus on protecting software as **copyrightable works** while explicitly excluding software from patentability.
- Users have specific **rights** over the software, such as correcting errors, making backup copies, and decompiling for interoperability, but these must not interfere with the author's legitimate rights.
- **Software licenses** are crucial in defining the rights and obligations of both creators and users.

---

## **Key Takeaways:**
- **Software** is a set of programs, procedures, and rules that perform tasks or solve problems, and it can be protected by **copyright**.
- **Copyright protection** applies to the **program** and its necessary components, excluding user manuals and ideas.
- **Software patents** are generally excluded, but software can be patented if it involves technical aspects, novelty, and industrial application.
- **Users** of software have specific rights, including correcting errors, making backups, and decompiling, under certain conditions.
- **Licensing** is essential to regulate software distribution and usage, ensuring both **rights** and **obligations** are respected.
