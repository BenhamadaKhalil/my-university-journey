## **Exercise 1**

**Question:** Among the following examples, which ones are projects?

|Example|Is it a Project?|Explanation|
|---|:-:|---|
|Building a house|✅ Yes|Unique, temporary, has clear goal and deadline.|
|Assembling a car (mass production in a factory)|❌ No|Repetitive routine operation, not a unique result.|
|Implementing a quality approach for certification|✅ Yes|Unique objective, limited duration, specific deliverable (certification).|
|Running an election campaign|✅ Yes|Temporary activity with defined beginning and end, unique outcomes.|

**Final Answer:**  
✅ Project: _Building a house_, _Implementing quality certification_, _Election campaign_  
❌ Not a project: _Assembling cars (continuous production)_

## **Exercise 2**

### **Project Context:**

- Time deadline: 1 year (must be ready before trade fair)
    
- Max budget: **600,000 DA** (cannot exceed)
    
- High quality required (competition is strong)
    

### **Constraint Ranking (Most Important → Least Important)**

|Rank|Constraint|Reason|
|---|---|---|
|**1st – Cost Constraint 💰**|The company **cannot exceed the allocated budget**.||
|**2nd – Quality Constraint ⭐**|The product must meet strict quality standards to compete.||
|**3rd – Time Constraint ⏱**|Important, but less critical than cost and quality in this case.||

**Final Answer:**  
**Cost > Quality > Time**

## **Exercise 4**

**Question:** How does the project manager know whether the migration mode is manual or automatic? And why?

### ✅ **Answer**

The project manager must be informed **through a written validation** in the **Project Specifications / Requirements Document** (_Cahier des Charges_).

### **Why?**

- To **avoid misunderstandings**
    
- To **plan workload and responsibilities correctly**
    
- To **estimate costs and deadlines accurately**
    
- To **officially assign who does what**
    

### **Final Answer (Concise):**

The migration choice must be **formalized and written** in the **requirements document**, because it impacts the **workload, planning, cost, and responsibilities** of each party.
